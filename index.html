Questions

Good to know.

Does it contain alcohol?

No. Elia Hair Mist is alcohol-free.

Which hair types is it for?

Every hair type: straight, wavy, curly, coily, and hair worn under a hijab or dupatta.

Will it make my hair grow?

No. Elia is a hair fragrance with caring ingredients. It will not grow your hair. It will make it smell lovely and feel soft.

How do I use it?

Hold the bottle about 20 cm away and mist over dry or damp hair. Reapply whenever you like.

Can I wear it with my perfume?

Yes. The warm sandalwood base layers well under most perfumes.
